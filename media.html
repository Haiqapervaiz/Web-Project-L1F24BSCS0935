How We Prepare a Cake

  1. Measure all ingredients.
  2. Mix the cake batter.
  3. Pour it into a baking pan.
  4. Bake until properly cooked.
  5. Allow the cake to cool.
  6. Add cream and decorations.
  7. Serve the fresh cake.
